Jamily Reunion

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

How Jamily Reunion handles data

Jamily Reunion is a free Windows companion app for streamers. It runs on the streamer's own PC. Chat, viewer profiles, and settings are stored in a local database on that PC.

There is no Jamily Reunion server. The developer does not receive or store your chat, viewer profiles, settings, or Google access tokens.

YouTube and Google

Jamily Reunion uses YouTube API Services when you connect your Google account. It reads live chat from your own broadcasts and can send chat messages as you, for example when replying to chat commands. Google access tokens are stored only on your PC.

You can disconnect Google in Jamily Reunion or revoke access at your Google Account permissions.

Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Jamily Reunion's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Twitch

When you connect Twitch, Jamily Reunion connects to your own Twitch account to read and send chat. Chat and related app data are stored locally on your PC.

Optional TikTok LIVE connection

If you use the optional TikTok LIVE connection, the TikTok username you enter is sent to the third-party service Euler Stream to connect to TikTok LIVE chat.

Updates

Jamily Reunion checks an update information file on this GitHub Pages site to see whether an app update is available.

Viewer profiles and your choices

Viewers can type !opt-out in chat to stop their profile from being saved. Streamers can delete viewer data in the app.

Selling, sharing, and advertising

Jamily Reunion does not sell data or use it for advertising. The developer does not operate a server that receives or stores your data. Information is sent to Google, YouTube, or Twitch only as needed for the connected chat features, and the TikTok username you enter is sent to Euler Stream only for the optional TikTok LIVE connection.
